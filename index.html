WHAT THE OFFICE DOES

Work becomes
part of the scene.

◉

Presence you can read

Working agents appear in their rooms. Confirmed idle or away states remove the occupant; room lights follow a configurable grace period.

◷

A day that unfolds

Dawn, day, dusk, and night follow a Bangkok-time schedule. A fixed exhibition background keeps a display in the mood you choose.

▤

A closer look at activity

Select people and rooms to inspect status and available task summaries in the product. Room labels and task counts add context at a glance.

▣

Made for a wall

Wall view minimizes controls for ambient displays. Live clocks, subtle character motion, and responsive lighting keep the office expressive.

◇

Honest about uncertainty

Stale or missing telemetry is marked unavailable. The display hides uncertain occupants instead of inventing activity.

⌁

A place for every specialty

Dedicated rooms and a shared Commons turn software delivery roles into an easy-to-read visual workplace.

Virtual Office is an activity display. The agent workflows described below happen in the connected agent ecosystem; this public showcase does not launch agents or connect to private systems.

A VISUAL LANGUAGE FOR ACTIVITY

Small signals.
Clear meaning.

The building tells a story without turning uncertainty into a claim.

Working

A current activity signal makes the agent visible and lights the room.

Idle / away

Confirmed inactivity hides the occupant. The lights fade after the configured delay.

Unavailable

Missing or stale signals hide the occupant and retain the last reliable light state.
